## Two things not to undo

**The ordination is gone from the manuscript but not from the record.** Figure
S3 was deleted and Section 3.4 no longer reports it. The outputs, the report and
the M22 registry rows all stay. It was pre-registered and it was run; choosing
not to report a null is normal, erasing the evidence that it happened is not.

**Day 0 is not an observed onset.** It is the midpoint between the first and
last dates spawn was recorded at a location. The manuscript now says so once,
with the spread. Do not quietly upgrade it to "onset" elsewhere.

## The writing standard

`WRITING_STYLE.md`, fourteen sections. The last three are the ones that matter
now, because they were derived from this manuscript rather than from published
advice:

- **§12 Opener monotony.** 22 of 91 paragraphs opened with "The". Now 5.
- **§13 The gnomic framing sentence.** Abstract subject, claim about the
  argument rather than the world, pronoun pointing at nothing. Eight found.
  Three of them were introduced by the §12 fix, which is the lesson: fixing one
  tell mechanically introduces another.

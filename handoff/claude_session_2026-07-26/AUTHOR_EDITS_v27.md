## Substantive corrections the author made

Three of these are ecological facts, not style, and must be carried into the
rewrite.

1. **Spawns are more rapid than three or four days** (comment 44). The current
   Introduction overstates the duration at a shoreline.
2. **Gulls do more than work the surface and strandline** (comment 70). They eat
   floating vegetation carrying eggs, and take herring pushed to the surface or
   into very shallow water.
3. **Piscivores are not confined to adults** (comment 69). The author assumes
   they focus on adults but expects they can use eggs too. The current text
   states the clean version as if established.

A fourth is about framing rather than fact: **the Strait of Georgia was chosen
because it has the most data on both the herring side and the bird side**
(comment 76), not for the ecological reasons the current paragraph gives.
